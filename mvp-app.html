How to take your measurements

The whole point: same way, every time. Consistency reads trends; variation only reads noise.

⚖️ The weigh-in
Morning — right after the bathroom, before coffee or food 1
Same scale, same hard floor (never carpet — it tilts readings) 2
Minimal clothing, the same amount every time 3
Enter it, then let it go 4

One day is noise. The app reads the 7-day trend — a jump after a salty dinner is water, not fat. You weigh, the app watches.

📏 The tape
Soft tape, pulled snug — never pressing in, parallel to the floor all the way around 1
Waist: at the navel, relaxed, at the end of a normal exhale — never sucked in 2
Same anatomical spot every time — mark it with a pen the first week 3
Measure twice; more than 2cm apart = re-measure 4
Neck · just under the larynx Hip · widest over the glutes Thigh · mid hip-to-knee Arm · mid shoulder-to-elbow, same flex state

The scale leads, the tape confirms shape — muscle moving while weight stalls is exactly what the tape is for.

📷 The photos
Same spot, same daylight, plain background — phone at chest height or propped, not hand-held angles 1
Front · side · back · ¾ — arms slightly away from the body, natural stance 2
Morning, minimal clothing, the same clothes each time 3

No posing, no filter, no crop — the app reads shape honestly, so the baseline has to be honest.

The triangle

Scale (trend) + tape (shape) + photos (the look) — three honest instruments. If weight stalls but the waist drops and the photos change, that's progress the scale can't say.

Want the gold standard? A DEXA scan ~once a quarter measures fat vs lean directly — optional, worth it once or twice a year.
